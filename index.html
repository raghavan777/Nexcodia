Quiz Arena

[ TECH QUIZ ]
Advanced 3–4 Members

Test your technical expertise across computer science and engineering domains through challenging timed questions and problem-solving rounds.

📋 EVENT RULES & OFFICIAL GUIDELINES ▼

👥 Team Composition

  • Each team must consist of 3–4 members.
  • Team members cannot be changed after registration.

📋 General Rules

  • The total duration of the event is 2 hours.
  • The quiz consists of 3 rounds.
  • The quizmaster's decision will be final and binding.
  • The use of mobile phones, smartwatches, or any electronic devices is strictly prohibited.
  • Any form of malpractice or misconduct will lead to disqualification.
ROUND 1 Pass the Code
  • All 3–4 registered team members can participate.
  • This is a team-based technical quiz round.
  • Qualified teams will proceed to Round 2 with 2 members.
ROUND 2 Tech Relay
  • Only 2 members from each qualified team can participate.
  • This round will assess technical knowledge, logical thinking, and teamwork.
  • Qualified teams will proceed to the final round with 1 member.
ROUND 3 The Tech Escape — Final Round
  • 1 member will represent the team in the final round.
  • The finalist will compete individually on behalf of the team.
  • The team with the highest final score will be declared the Winner.

The Dooms Paper

[ PAPER PRESENTATION ]
Intermediate 1–4 Members

Create and present innovative technical ideas, research concepts, and technological solutions using impactful slides to impress judges and audience.

📋 EVENT RULES & OFFICIAL GUIDELINES ▼

👥 1. Team

  • A team can have 1 to 4 members.
  • All members must register before the deadline.
  • Bring your College ID Card.

📄 2. Paper Submission

  • Submit your paper in PDF format before the deadline.
  • Late submission may not be accepted.
  • Your paper must be your own work.
  • Do not copy from others or use false information.

⏱️ 3. Presentation

  • Each team gets 2–3 minutes to present. Do not exceed the given time.
  • Explain your main idea, how it works, important points and conclusion.
  • Judges may ask questions after your presentation.
  • Every team member should know about the paper.

💻 4. Laptop & Presentation

  • You can bring your own laptop.
  • Laptop will be provided by the organizers if needed.
  • Tell the coordinator in advance if you need a laptop.
  • Keep your presentation ready in PPT/PPTX and PDF.
  • Keep a backup copy of your presentation.

⚖️ 5. Discipline

  • Be respectful and well-behaved.
  • Come to the venue before your presentation time.
  • Keep silent when another team is presenting.
  • Keep your mobile phone on silent mode.
  • Do not touch or change technical equipment without permission.
  • Follow the instructions given by the organizers.

T04
🔐

The Final Escape

[ ESCAPE THE LAB ]
Intermediate 3–4 Members

Solve logical puzzles and coding challenges to unlock clues and escape the virtual laboratory before time runs out. Work smart and fast!

📋 EVENT RULES & OFFICIAL GUIDELINES ▼

🧪 Event Overview

  • Format: 4 progressive tech-themed rounds — complete them all to escape the lab!
  • Team Size: 3 to 4 members per team across all rounds.
  • General: Judges' decision will be final in all rounds.
ROUND 1 Pixel Play — Tech Pictionary
  • Team Size: 3 to 4 members.
  • Drawing: One member draws; teammates guess the tech term.
  • Restrictions: No talking, letters, or numbers while drawing.
  • Time Limit: Each word has a 60-second time limit.
  • Words: Tech-related terms, picked randomly.
  • Rule: Only the designated drawer may draw.
  • Scoring: Correct guess within time = 1 point.
  • Rotation: Drawer rotates every turn.
  • Support: System / whiteboard will be provided.
ROUND 2 Circuit Spy — Tech Imposter
  • Team Size: 3 to 4 members.
  • Setup: All members except one get the same tech term; the imposter secretly gets a fake term.
  • Clues: Each member gives one clue about their term; the imposter must bluff to avoid detection.
  • Discussion: Team discusses for 60 seconds after all clues are given.
  • Voting: One vote decides the suspected imposter.
  • Scoring: Correct guess = team point; undetected imposter = imposter point.
  • Format: Only one round per game.
ROUND 3 Guess the Geek — Who Am I
  • Team Size: 3 to 4 members.
  • Format: Clues about a tech figure or company are read one by one, from hardest to easiest.
  • Buzzing: Teams may buzz in anytime to answer.
  • Wrong Answer: Locks the team out for that round.
  • Scoring: Points decrease with each clue used.
  • Rule: No discussion allowed after buzzing in.
  • Timing: 10 seconds given per clue if no team buzzes.
  • System: Buzzer / hand-raise system will be used.
ROUND 4 Byte Hunt — Treasure Hunt
  • Team Size: 3 to 4 members.
  • Checkpoints: 5 to 8 tech-themed checkpoints; each clue must be solved to reach the next.
  • Start: Teams start at staggered intervals.
  • Rule: No skipping checkpoints — volunteers verify answers at each stop.
  • Time Limit: 30 to 40 minutes total.
  • Phones: Allowed only for scanning QR codes.
  • Winner: Fastest team to finish wins.

🔐 FINAL COMMAND

DRAW IT SPY IT GUESS IT HUNT IT ESCAPE!

THE FINAL ESCAPE — Only the sharpest minds break out of the lab.

Cosmic Clash

[ E-SPORTS / FREE FIRE ]
All Levels Squad / Solo

Enter the digital battleground! Compete in intense Free Fire & gaming tournaments, demonstrate team coordination, and claim ultimate supremacy.

📋 EVENT RULES & OFFICIAL GUIDELINES ▼

🏆 Tournament Format — 3 Stage System

STAGE 1 BR Qualifiers
  • Match 1 — BR: All registered squads compete in a Battle Royale match.
  • Match 2 — BR: Second qualifier Battle Royale match for all squads.
  • Qualification Criteria: Top 2 teams from each match qualify to Stage 2.
  • Kill Threshold: A minimum of 8 kills is required to qualify for the next round.
STAGE 2 CS Semi-Finals
  • Match 3 — CS: Team 1 vs Team 2 — Classic Squad clash.
  • Match 4 — CS: Team 3 vs Team 4 — Classic Squad clash.
  • Qualification: Winners of each match advance to the Grand Final.
STAGE 3 CS Grand Final
  • Match 5 — CS: Winner 1 vs Winner 2 — The ultimate showdown.
  • Champion: The winner of Match 5 becomes the Tournament Champion.

📊 Match Progression

Match 1 (BR) → Match 3 (CS) → GRAND FINAL
Match 2 (BR) → Match 4 (CS) → 🏆 CHAMPION

⚙️ Main Rules

  • Rule 1 — Gun Skin: Gun Skin must be turned OFF in both BR and CS matches.
  • Rule 2 — No Team-Up: Teaming up with any other team or opponent during BR matches is strictly PROHIBITED.
  • Rule 3 — Kill Threshold: A minimum of 8 kills is required to qualify from the BR Qualifier stage to the next round.
  • Rule 4 — Character Skill: Character Skills must be turned OFF in all CS matches.

🛡️ Fair Play & Conduct

  • Device Policy: Participants must use their own mobile devices. Tablets are not permitted.
  • Account Authenticity: Only the participant's own Free Fire account may be used. Account sharing is prohibited.
  • Hacking / Cheating: Use of any hacks, mods, emulators, or third-party tools will result in immediate disqualification.
  • Connectivity: Players are responsible for their own internet connectivity. Disconnection will not result in a rematch.
  • Organizer's Word is Final: All decisions made by the organizing committee are final and binding.

🎮 FINAL COMMAND

SQUAD UP 8 KILLS TO SURVIVE PLAY FAIR CLAIM VICTORY

Cosmic Clash — Only the deadliest squad rises to be Champion.

Infinity Bid Wars

[ IPL AUCTION ]
Strategy 2–4 Members

Experience the thrill of a real IPL auction! Strategy, bidding wars, budget management, and squad building. Outbid rivals to assemble the ultimate dream team.

📋 EVENT RULES & OFFICIAL GUIDELINES ▼

📋 Rules & Regulations

  • Team Size: Each team must consist of 2–4 participants, with one member designated as the Team Captain.
  • Materials: Participants must bring their own pen, paper, laptop, calculator, and other required materials. The Organizing Committee will not provide these.
  • Auction Purse: Each team will receive an equal stipulated purse and must manage it strategically throughout the auction.
  • Bidding: Bids must follow the announced bidding increments and will be considered final once acknowledged by the auctioneer.
  • Squad Formation: Teams must form the required playing line-up within the stipulated purse. Failure to do so will result in disqualification.
  • Judging Criteria: The judging criteria will remain confidential and will be revealed only after the auction concludes.
  • Fair Play: Collusion, fake bids, disruptive behaviour, or any form of unfair practice will lead to penalties or disqualification.
  • Disputes: Any disputes must be raised through the Team Captain. The decision of the Organizing Committee will be final and binding.
  • General: Participants must report to the venue on time and follow all instructions given by the organizers.
